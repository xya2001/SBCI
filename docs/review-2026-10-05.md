# Response to the implementation review of 5 October 2026

The review ("SBCI implementation review", 5 October 2026, of v0.0.1.dev0 at
commit 4b1850b) built the wheel, ran the suite and then tested the
mathematics rather than the agreement with MATLAB. It reported eleven
findings and three documentation points. This note says what was done about
each; PORTING.md item 8 has the causes and the measured sizes, and the
commit that answers the review carries the code, the tests and the documents.
Nine further lists followed, the last two reviewing the notebooks; then a
recheck of everything, the answers to what it left open and an eleventh
list. Each has its section below.

**Every finding was real.** Each was reproduced with independent probes
before anything was changed. Three of them -- the Legendre derivative
recurrence, the normalization order of the transported square-root density,
and the projection objective -- are errors in the MATLAB references that the
port had reproduced to rounding, which is exactly what the agreement tests
measured and could not see past. The package now computes the right thing
by default and keeps the reference arithmetic behind `reference=True`
(ENCORE and `project`) and `strict_upstream=True` (ConSEAL), so that the
comparisons with the references still run; the documents say which
agreement figures hold only under those flags.

## The documentation points

- *ConSEAL paper equivalence.* The settings `delta=0.1, step_clamp=inf,
  viscosity=0, threshold=1e-6` are now described as the unregularized public
  update, not the paper's: the fork also composes the vertex map directly and
  differentiates the kernel its own way, which the package does not
  reproduce (USAGE.md, PORTING.md item 7).
- *Migration to MSMAll data.* USAGE.md qualifies the example: a group warp is
  a template-coordinate operation; a subject's MSMAll maps also went through
  that subject's own registration, which has to be composed first
  (`SphereMap.from_gifti(native_sphere, msmall_sphere)`).
- *Verification language.* README.md no longer calls the methods "verified
  ports"; VERIFICATION.md opens its list of things to challenge with
  "agreement with MATLAB is not correctness", names the three shared errors,
  and lists the independent invariants the suite now asserts.

## What the recheck left open

The recheck ended with five things left open. One is settled here and one
corrected, and the comparison behind a third is running; the area-weighted
objective (finding 6) waits for WP1, and whether to offer an unrestricted PCA
beside the functional one is a decision for the package's maintainers.

- **The scores (finding 1).** The fit recorded each component's scores one
  alternating step before its final vector, as the reference's dense path
  does. This note and PORTING.md had put the gap at the fit's tolerance,
  1e-3; on real data it is about the square root of it, 1.6% to 2.0% of the
  largest score on the prediction notebook's folds. Since 7 October the fit
  scores the vectors it returns and deflates with those, with no switch back,
  and `project()` returns the training scores to rounding at any tolerance.
  PORTING.md item 17 has the measurements and what it changed. On the 943
  young adults of RESULTS.md sex now shows in 13 of 20 components rather than
  11 (the strongest at adjusted p 1e-7, not 2e-8), and fluid intelligence
  still in none; the notebooks' conclusions stand, their numbers moving in
  the third decimal (the components' AUC for sex 0.658 to 0.662, the
  separable form's cost 0.169 to 0.164).
- **Finding 3's size.** The table's "0.001 degrees" was 0.0013 radians on the
  test's coarse ico2 grid, 0.07 degrees. On ico4 one relocation moves an
  endpoint 0.0012 degrees at most; the row and *Still open* now say so, and
  why.
- **Two component numbers in USAGE.md** came from the inference probe's
  printout, which counted from zero: the strongest sex component is 16, not
  15, and the age contrast's is 5, not 4. The probe now counts from one, as
  the documents do.
- **The paper's own ConSEAL.** Before deciding, the author's research fork was
  run in MATLAB on the same subjects as the package: three known warps, five
  subjects' split halves and a real pair. Its pair run reproduces the fork's
  logged run of 6 May to the last printed digit (0.291389 to 0.199431 in 460
  iterations). The comparison is being scored; the decision follows in its
  own commit.

## The eleventh list

A further list found seven bugs that change results and eight failures on
less common paths. All hold; PORTING.md item 18 has the verdicts, the fixes
and the sizes, and every fix comes with a test that fails before it.

- **Results:** permutation p-values on discrete scores count tied
  permutations (the same scores rescaled gave 0.022 to 0.050 where the exact
  answer over the same draws is 0.058); the FC exchange file averages
  correlations over cortex only (the 407 fsLR vertices beside the medial
  wall were scaled by 0.125 to 0.889); the documented atlas-level coupling
  takes SC's mean density, as FC's side is a mean correlation (0.329 to 0.421
  on the eleven, where SC's mass gave the quoted 0.271 to 0.370; the review's
  0.566 and 0.668 were not reproduced); ConSEAL's `area_weighted=True`
  registers (raw areas had shrunk the cost 41,666-fold, below the threshold);
  its rigid search turns about z (8 degrees about z had been left 8 to 9
  degrees off); the fsaverage sphere file takes grid vertex `i` as fsaverage
  vertex `i` (27 vertices had been half a degree off); and an endpoint index
  past its hemisphere is refused, not read back in the other one.
- **Less common paths:** a supplied grid on the axis is turned clear of it and
  the turn recorded (the advised hand turn, 17 degrees on the bundled sphere,
  was lost to `migrate_warp`); a density that is not symmetric is refused;
  global coupling keeps one-vertex regions; the ARPACK fallback runs on SciPy
  1.10; `.H5` files load; the exchange file is written whole or not at all; a
  single colour bound is mirrored about zero; and the release tools carry the
  HCP's terms, with no CC-BY licence.

## Still open

- Whether the functional PCA should minimize the area-weighted approximation
  error (finding 6) is a method decision for WP1; the current objective is
  the reference's and is now described as such.
- A warped object registered again onto itself still moves up to 0.0012
  degrees on ico4 (finding 3), as a fresh one does: the reset is fixed, and
  what is left is the relocation every registration makes. An endpoint's
  weights come from the closest point on its flat triangle, as in the
  reference's libigl, and its position from the ray through that point, so a
  round trip through the identity moves it slightly toward the triangle's
  edges. It adds up only for an object registered again and again: 0.012
  degrees after ten, 0.12 after a hundred.
- The paper's own ConSEAL update (direct vertex-map composition, its
  derivative machinery) is not implemented; the package ports the public code.
