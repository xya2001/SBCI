Continuous connectomes of $subjects HCP Young Adult subjects: terms of use
=========================================================================

These files are structural and functional continuous connectomes on the fsaverage
ico4 grid (5,124 vertices). They were derived from data of the WU-Minn Human
Connectome Project (HCP Young Adult, 1200 Subjects release) with the tools of the
sbci Python package (https://github.com/xya2001/SBCI); PROCESSING.md says how.

They are redistributed under the WU-Minn HCP Consortium Open Access Data Use
Terms, which apply to anyone who downloads them:
https://www.humanconnectome.org/study/hcp-young-adult/document/wu-minn-hcp-consortium-open-access-data-use-terms

In short, by using these files you agree to those terms. Among them:

- do not attempt to identify or contact any subject;
- follow your institution's rules for research on human subjects;
- redistribute these data, or anything derived from them, only under the same
  terms;
- acknowledge the HCP in any public presentation of results, with this wording:

  "Data were provided [in part] by the Human Connectome Project, WU-Minn
  Consortium (Principal Investigators: David Van Essen and Kamil Ugurbil;
  1U54MH091657) funded by the 16 NIH Institutes and Centers that support the
  NIH Blueprint for Neuroscience Research; and by the McDonnell Center for
  Systems Neuroscience at Washington University."

- cite the publications that describe how the HCP acquired and processed the
  data used; README.md names two.

The terms page above is authoritative; this note only points to it.
